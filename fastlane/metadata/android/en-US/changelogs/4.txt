New in this version:
- Go back and edit any past day in History: change what medication you took, and your symptoms, joints, and notes.
- Back up all your data to one file, and restore it later. Everything stays on your device.
- Export your log as a CSV spreadsheet, or a plain PDF report to keep or show someone. A record only, with no interpretation.
- An optional app lock, using your phone's own unlock (fingerprint, face, PIN, or pattern).
- A new Painful option on the body map, alongside Sore and Swollen.
