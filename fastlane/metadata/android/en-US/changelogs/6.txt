New in this version:
- You can now set up the body map to match your own body. Add your own body parts, including a single finger or toe, rename them, reorder them, hide the ones you do not use, or remove them.
- Add and reorder your own categories too, alongside the built in ones.
- Hiding a part keeps everything you have already recorded. Removing a part, or a category with parts in it, asks first and tells you how many recorded marks would go.
- A Restore default parts button brings back any of the built in parts you have removed.
