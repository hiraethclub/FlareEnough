# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 18 September 2026_

Flare Enough is built so that your health information stays yours. This policy
explains, in plain English, what the app does and does not do with your data.

## The short version

- Flare Enough does not collect any of your data.
- Flare Enough does not transmit any of your data anywhere.
- The app has no internet permission at all. It cannot send data off your device,
  even by accident.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no trackers, no advertising, and no third
  party services of any kind.

## Where your data lives

Everything you enter, your medications, doses, symptoms, notes, tags, flare days,
and stillness sessions, is stored only on your device, in the app's own private
storage. No one else can read it, and it is never uploaded.

## When data leaves your device

Your data only ever leaves your device when you choose to send it, using your
device's own sharing tools. This can happen in three ways, all started by you:

1. Backup export: you save a copy of your data to a file you choose.
2. CSV export: you save your dose and symptom records as a spreadsheet file.
3. Appointment report: you create a PDF to share with a healthcare professional.

In each case you pick where the file goes. Once a file leaves the app, it is
outside the app's control, so please keep exported files somewhere safe.

You are responsible for keeping your own backups. If you uninstall the app or
lose your device without a backup, the data cannot be recovered, because it was
never stored anywhere else.

## Permissions

The app asks only for permissions it needs to remind you reliably, and each is
explained inside the app before your device asks. None of them allow the app to
access the internet. See [PERMISSIONS.md](PERMISSIONS.md) for the full list and
the reason for each.

## Optional app lock

You can turn on an app lock that uses your device's own biometrics or PIN. This
is handled by your device. Flare Enough never sees or stores your biometric data
or your PIN.

## Children

The app is a personal health tool and does not target children. It collects no
data from anyone.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in the app's public
repository with a new date at the top.

## Contact

Flare Enough is free and open source. Questions and issues can be raised in the
project's public repository.

## Not medical advice

Flare Enough records and reminds only. It does not diagnose, recommend treatments,
adjust doses, check drug interactions, or interpret symptoms. For medical advice,
diagnosis, or treatment, please speak to a qualified healthcare professional.
